Your privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Petalune saves your game on your device. There are no adverts, advertising trackers, analytics SDKs or Petalune accounts. We do not operate a cloud-save service.

Who is responsible?

Petalune is developed by Thomas Williams, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom. In this policy, “I”, “me” and “my” refer to Thomas Williams, the controller of personal information handled for Petalune support. Contact: petaluneapp.support@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Petalune iOS app and this privacy and support website.

Information on your device

The game stores progress, character and world choices, settings and local save backups on your device so that you can continue playing. Local technical information, such as performance measurements and purchase-check timing, may also be stored on the device. Petalune does not send these game saves or local performance records to me.

You do not need to provide your real name, email address or create an account to play. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone. It does not sell personal information or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Apple downloads and purchases

If Petalune has a download price, Apple handles that payment. If in-app purchases are offered, they are processed through Apple’s App Store. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit service to check purchase and entitlement information on your device, such as the purchased product, transaction identifier, purchase date, and expiry or refund status, to unlock content and restore eligible purchases. These records are not sent to a Petalune server.

I do not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles your Apple Account, payment processing and its own transaction records under Apple’s privacy policy. Apple may make sales reports and diagnostics available to developers through its services; these are separate from the game’s local saves and depend on Apple’s services and your device privacy settings.

Fonts used by the app

The current app requests its display fonts from Google Fonts when a connection is available. Those requests disclose your IP address and technical request information to Google so that it can deliver the fonts. This is a connection for displaying text; Petalune does not include Google Analytics. See Google’s privacy policy. This support website uses system fonts and does not request Google Fonts.

If you contact me

If you email petaluneapp.support@gmail.com, I receive your email address, your name if included, your message and any attachments you choose to send. I use this information to respond, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. Please send only what is needed, and avoid including passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

My lawful basis under UK data protection law for handling ordinary support messages is legitimate interests: helping players and resolving problems. Where processing is necessary to meet a legal obligation, such as handling a data-rights request, that obligation is the lawful basis. Providing support information is voluntary, but without enough information I may be unable to resolve your request. I do not use support emails for marketing or automated decision-making.

Service providers and this website

Support email is handled using Gmail, provided by Google. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security, including when visitors are not signed in. See the GitHub privacy statement. I have not added cookies, advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms or embedded social media to this site.

Google, GitHub and Apple may process information outside the UK under their respective privacy arrangements, including applicable adequacy decisions or contractual safeguards described in their policies. Contact me for information about the arrangements relevant to your support correspondence. I do not send gameplay records to advertising or analytics providers. Service providers handling personal information on my behalf must protect it in accordance with applicable data protection law. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to establish or defend legal rights.

Keeping and deleting information

Your local game data remains on your device until removed. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app data. This also removes local progress, which I cannot recover. Device or iCloud backups may contain app data depending on your Apple settings; you can manage these backups through Apple. Restoring a device backup may restore saved data. Removing the app does not delete Apple’s purchase records or cancel any subscription.

I keep support correspondence only as long as necessary to resolve the enquiry, provide relevant follow-up and deal with any related dispute or legal obligation. The retention period depends on the issue, whether it is resolved and whether there is a continuing legal need. You can request deletion by email. Google, GitHub and Apple retain information they control according to their own policies.

Your privacy rights

Under UK data protection law, depending on the circumstances, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information I hold about you, restrict its use, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or request a portable copy where that right applies. If processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

To make a request or raise a privacy concern, email petaluneapp.support@gmail.com. I may need enough information to verify your identity. I normally respond within one month; where the law permits an extension, I will explain it. I cannot access or remotely erase game saves stored only on your device.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or by writing to the Information Commissioner’s Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF. You do not have to contact me first.

Children and families

The game does not require children to provide personal contact information to play. Parents or guardians should handle support enquiries and purchase decisions for children. If you believe a child has sent me personal information that should be deleted, please contact me.

Changes to this policy

I will update this page when the app’s features or privacy practices change and revise the date above. Where required, significant changes will also be brought to your attention in the app.

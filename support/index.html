Petalune support

Need a hand with your game? Contact Thomas Williams, Petalune’s independent developer.

Email support
petaluneapp.support@gmail.com

Reporting a problem

Please include your device model, iOS version, Petalune version and a short description of what happened. A screenshot can help, but remove any personal information first. Never send your Apple password or payment details.

Saved progress

Your game is saved on your device. Petalune does not have its own cloud-save service, and I cannot retrieve your local progress. Please contact support before deleting the app to troubleshoot, because deletion removes local game data.

Purchases and billing

Apple handles App Store payments. For a refund request, visit Apple’s Report a Problem service. If subscriptions are offered, manage or cancel them through your Apple Account’s subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Privacy enquiries

Use the same email address for privacy questions or data-rights requests. Read the privacy policy for details of how information is handled.

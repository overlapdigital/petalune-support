# Petalune

Welcome to the official privacy and support pages for **Petalune**, an independent game by Thomas Williams.

## Help and support

Need a hand or want to report a problem? Visit the [support page](https://overlapdigital.github.io/petalune-support/support/) or email [petaluneapp.support@gmail.com](mailto:petaluneapp.support@gmail.com).

When reporting a problem, please include your device model, iOS version, Petalune version and a short description of what happened. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

## Your saved game

Petalune stores your progress on your device and does not have its own cloud-save service. Deleting the app removes its local game data, so please contact support before deleting it to troubleshoot a problem.

## Your privacy

Petalune has no adverts or analytics SDKs, and you do not need a Petalune account to play. Read the [privacy policy](https://overlapdigital.github.io/petalune-support/privacy/) for details about local saves, Apple purchases, support emails and services used by the app and website.
